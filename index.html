Proximity

Closer than 1 m to another agent → the one closing in faster stops. A visitor closer than 1 m to an artwork → the nearest staff comes over and the visitor steps back.

Screens

A visitor within 2.5 m of a screen and fewer than 20 in the zone → the screen turns ON.

Axioms

    Controls

    Click an agent or screen to open its window · drag agents to move them · click the ground to probe or set B · hover to describe a spot · right-drag to pan · scroll to zoom · Space pauses · Esc closes a window.

    Movement

    → toward ← away from ↻ around a screen, an artwork or another agent. Lines are drawn for agents whose window is open.

    Exploration

    Visitors start knowing nothing. They see through a 120° cone, 6 m long, and map what they see. Run 1 explores; later runs follow three interests. Open a visitor's window to see its map: fog where it has not looked, its trail, ticked doors, ringed finds and numbered stops.

    Marks

    Ring on the floor: 2.5 m screen range · wedge: in front of a screen · ⏸: stopped for personal space · dashed amber line: staff on the way · ▸| one-way door (check valve) · S1–S3: shortcuts (S1 staff only).
